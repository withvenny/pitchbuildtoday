# 00. Problems to Ideas

## Purpose

This step converts an early problem statement, rough founder thought, voice note, transcript, sketch, or unstructured product signal into a usable idea artifact.

The goal is not to perfect the product yet. The goal is to create enough clarity for the next step in the process.

## Accepted Inputs

The agent may ingest raw notes, founder monologues, meeting transcripts, sketches, screenshots, research fragments, customer complaints, competitor references, or any other early material that helps describe the problem being explored.

The input does not need to be clean. This step exists because early product thinking is usually incomplete.

## Transformation

The agent is responsible for reading the input, identifying the actual problem, extracting useful product signals, removing noise, and organizing the material into a flexible idea artifact.

The agent should preserve ambiguity where needed, but it should not pass confusion forward when the confusion can be clarified through structure.

## Output

The output is an idea artifact that gives the next step enough context to create a product brief.

The output should capture the core problem, early audience signals, possible product directions, assumptions, constraints, and open questions without forcing the idea into a final product shape too early.

## Output Format

The preferred output is a simple written artifact that can be saved as `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`, or another format suitable for review and handoff.

The format should remain flexible because the purpose of this step is clarity, not formatting theater.

## Gate

This step is complete when the idea can be understood by someone who did not participate in the original conversation.

The next step should not have to guess what problem is being explored, who may be affected by it, or why the idea is worth shaping into a product brief.
