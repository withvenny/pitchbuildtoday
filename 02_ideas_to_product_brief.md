# 02. Ideas to Product Brief

## Purpose

This step converts a clear idea artifact into a product brief.

The goal is to move from early idea clarity into a more usable product direction that can support backlog creation, planning, and future build decisions.

## Accepted Inputs

The agent may ingest the idea artifact from the previous step, along with any supporting notes, assumptions, audience signals, constraints, open questions, or early product directions.

The input should provide enough context for the agent to understand the problem being explored and why it may deserve a product.

## Transformation

The agent is responsible for organizing the idea into a concise product brief.

The agent should define the product direction, clarify the intended audience, describe the core problem, frame the proposed offering, and identify the early boundaries of the product without overbuilding the concept.

## Output

The output is a product brief that gives the next step enough context to create a backlog.

The product brief should help a reader understand what is being built, who it is for, why it matters, what the first version should focus on, and what should remain outside the current scope.

## Output Format

The preferred output is a written product brief that can be saved as `.md`, `.pdf`, `.doc`, or another format suitable for review and handoff.

The format should remain simple enough to support planning, collaboration, and downstream agent use.

## Gate

This step is complete when the product direction can be understood without revisiting the raw idea materials.

The next step should not have to guess the audience, core problem, proposed offering, MVP boundary, or primary product intent.
